# AI Student Doubt Portal

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI Student Doubt Portal is an AI-powered web application designed to help students get quick and simple answers to their academic questions.

The system allows students to enter their doubts through a user-friendly interface and receive AI-generated explanations. The project is designed to make learning easier by providing instant assistance without requiring students to wait for a teacher or search through multiple resources.

The portal can be extended with features such as OCR-based question recognition, voice input, Tamil-English translation, and a teacher dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of the AI Student Doubt Portal are:

- To provide instant answers to students' academic questions.
- To make learning more interactive and accessible.
- To use Artificial Intelligence for automated doubt clarification.
- To support students in understanding difficult concepts through simple explanations.
- To provide a user-friendly platform for asking questions.
- To support future multilingual and voice-based learning features.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. AI-Based Doubt Solving
Students can enter their academic questions and receive AI-generated answers and explanations.

### 2. Simple and User-Friendly Interface
The portal provides an easy-to-use interface where students can enter their doubts and view the generated answers.

### 3. Voice Input
The system can be extended to support voice-based questions, allowing students to ask doubts using their voice.

### 4. OCR Support
OCR technology can be used to extract text from images. Students can take or upload an image of a question and convert it into text for AI processing.

### 5. Tamil-English Language Support
The portal can support Tamil-to-English and English-to-Tamil translation, making the system more accessible to students who prefer regional languages.

### 6. Instant Responses
The AI system processes the student's question and provides a response without requiring manual intervention.

### 7. Future Teacher Dashboard
A teacher dashboard can be added to monitor student questions and provide additional support.
